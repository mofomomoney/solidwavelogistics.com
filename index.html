What we do

Last-mile delivery.
Fully managed.

SolidWave Logistics partners with businesses that depend on reliable last-mile delivery operations. As a dedicated delivery subcontractor, we provide the fleet, qualified Class A CDL drivers, and operational management necessary to execute contracted routes.

  • Contracted route coverage

    We support businesses with recurring last-mile delivery routes, providing the resources and coordination necessary to fulfill agreed service requirements.

  • Fleet & driver solutions

    Every delivery operation has different requirements. We provide suitable vehicles and equipment along with qualified Class A CDL drivers, aligning our resources with each customer's operational needs.

  • Daily operations management

    We manage driver coordination, equipment readiness, and route coverage to support consistent execution and operational accountability.

Why subcontract
your routes to SolidWave?

Managing delivery routes requires more than having trucks available. It requires qualified drivers, suitable equipment, daily coordination, and the ability to respond to changing operational demands. SolidWave brings these responsibilities together under one managed operation, so you can focus on your broader delivery network while we handle the fleet and drivers supporting your assigned routes.

  • Less fleet and driver management in-house
  • A dedicated operational partner
  • Consistent route coverage
  • Equipment matched to each contract
  • Professionally managed Class A drivers
  • Clear operational accountability
